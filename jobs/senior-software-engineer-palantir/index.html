Senior Software Engineer

SAIC · Requisition 2614411

Build and sustain mission software for Space C2, using Palantir Foundry to connect operational data and support decisions across domains.

Discuss this role ↗

What you’ll do

  • Develop Foundry pipelines, ontologies, applications, and data integrations.
  • Design modular architectures and deliver software through Agile and DevSecOps practices.
  • Support deployment, sustainment, and technical documentation.

What you’ll bring

  • Relevant computing or information-systems degree and the experience shown.

  • Foundry, Gotham, or comparable platform experience; APIs, data modeling, and software lifecycle skills.
  • Active Top Secret clearance, eligibility for SCI/SAP access, and willingness to consent to a polygraph.
