Launch integration

Space Launch Systems Integrator

Waypoint Sierra Consultants

Own the spacecraft-to-launch-vehicle interface for National Security Space Launch missions, from early requirements through payload processing and launch operations.

Discuss this role ↗

What you’ll do

  • Develop integration requirements and control spacecraft / launch-vehicle interfaces.
  • Coordinate satellite program offices, launch providers, and government acquisition teams.
  • Manage statements of work, engineering changes, mission costs, and schedule dependencies.
  • Lead integration meetings and brief senior government and military leaders on technical status and risk.

What you’ll bring

  • Engineering bachelor’s degree; master’s preferred.
  • Direct ownership of interface documents, requirements negotiation, and launch integration.
  • Experience with payload processing facilities, launch campaigns, statements of work, and engineering change proposals.
  • Ability to work in a classified facility in El Segundo and brief senior leadership.
  • The supplied description prefers an active clearance and also considers inactive clearances or eligibility; confirm current program requirements with the recruiter.

Helpful experience

  • Experience with multiple launch vehicles and coordination across propulsion, structures, avionics, ground systems, and range operations.
