FAQ

Common questions before getting started.

What kinds of companies do you work with?

Primarily startup founders scaling fast and growth-stage executives (VP/C-suite) who've hit a ceiling. If you have a critical objective and need firepower to hit it, we're a good fit.

How fast can you assemble a team?

Weeks, not quarters. We move at the speed your mission demands. The exact timeline depends on the roles and complexity, but urgency is built into how we operate.

Do you replace our existing team or augment it?

Augment. We work alongside your people, filling gaps and adding capabilities your current team doesn't have. We're not here to replace anyone—we're here to make the mission succeed.

What does a typical engagement look like?

It starts with a conversation. We learn your objective, assess what's needed, and propose an approach. Most clients start with one capability and expand as trust builds.

How is this different from a staffing agency or consultancy?

Staffing agencies fill seats. Consultancies deliver decks. We assemble mission-critical teams, build executable strategies, and deploy AI systems that produce measurable results. We stay in the fight with you.
